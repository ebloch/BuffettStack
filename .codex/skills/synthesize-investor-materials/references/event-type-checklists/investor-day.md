# Investor Day — Event Checklist

**Event Characteristics:**
- Held every 2-3 years (major strategic events)
- Multi-hour, multi-presenter format
- Deep dives into strategy, segments, and long-term vision
- Often includes facility tours, product demos
- Management showcase (executives beyond CEO/CFO)

**Primary Focus:** Long-term strategic direction and multi-year targets

---

## Mandatory Extraction

### Strategy & Vision

- [ ] **Multi-year targets** — revenue, margins, EPS growth rates
  - Timeline (e.g., "by 2027", "over next 3 years")
  - Baseline year for comparison
- [ ] **TAM/SAM/SOM framework** — market sizing and share targets
  - Total Addressable Market
  - Serviceable Addressable Market
  - Target market share
- [ ] **Strategic priorities** — ranked list with evidence of commitment
- [ ] **Investment areas** — where is capital being deployed
- [ ] **Secular trends** — what macro forces management believes favor them
- [ ] **Competitive moat assertions** — how they claim to win

### Financial Targets

- [ ] **Revenue growth targets** — CAGR or specific year targets
- [ ] **Margin targets** — operating margin, EBITDA margin expansion
- [ ] **EPS growth targets** — if given
- [ ] **Free cash flow targets** — conversion or absolute
- [ ] **Return on capital targets** — ROIC, ROE
- [ ] **Efficiency initiatives** — cost savings programs, synergies

### Capital Allocation

- [ ] **Capital return framework** — dividend + buyback policies
- [ ] **M&A appetite** — acquisition criteria, focus areas, deal sizes
- [ ] **CapEx outlook** — growth vs. maintenance, major projects
- [ ] **Balance sheet targets** — leverage ratios, cash position

### Segment Deep Dives

For each segment presented:
- [ ] **Growth drivers** — what's fueling expansion
- [ ] **Competitive position** — market share, ranking claims
- [ ] **Key initiatives** — specific projects, product launches
- [ ] **Financial targets** — segment-specific goals
- [ ] **Management talent** — who leads it, their background

### Management Showcase

- [ ] **Leadership team presentations** — who presented beyond CEO/CFO
- [ ] **Succession depth signals** — are next-generation leaders visible
- [ ] **Culture/values messaging** — how they describe company culture
- [ ] **Hiring plans** — headcount growth areas

---

## Red Flag Triggers

Watch for and flag if present:

- [ ] Targets significantly above historical performance without clear catalysts
- [ ] TAM inflation (using broadest possible market definition)
- [ ] Vague "long-term" targets without specific timelines
- [ ] Missing segment-level breakdowns for consolidated targets
- [ ] Competitive claims without supporting data
- [ ] M&A dependency in growth targets without pipeline visibility
- [ ] Same targets restated from prior Investor Day (no progress)
- [ ] Key executives missing from lineup (departure signals?)

---

## Quality Signals

Positive signs to note:

- [ ] Specific, measurable targets with clear timelines
- [ ] Historical track record shown against prior targets
- [ ] Balanced discussion of risks and challenges
- [ ] Deep bench of management presenters
- [ ] Customer testimonials or case studies
- [ ] Detailed competitive positioning with data
- [ ] Clear capital allocation framework

---

## Context Questions

Answer these in the synthesis:

1. What's genuinely new vs. restatement of known strategy?
2. Are the targets achievable based on historical performance?
3. How does this compare to competitor positioning?
4. What's the biggest risk to hitting these targets?
5. Did management address prior commitments (did they deliver)?
