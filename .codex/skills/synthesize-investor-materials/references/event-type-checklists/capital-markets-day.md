# Capital Markets Day — Event Checklist

**Event Characteristics:**
- Annual or bi-annual (more frequent than Investor Day)
- Focus on financial execution and near-term outlook
- CFO typically co-leads or leads
- Emphasis on capital allocation, returns, efficiency
- Often coincides with annual guidance refresh

**Primary Focus:** Financial targets, capital allocation, and operational efficiency

---

## Mandatory Extraction

### Financial Targets

- [ ] **Revenue guidance** — next fiscal year
  - Organic growth vs. M&A contribution
  - Geographic/segment breakdown if given
- [ ] **Margin guidance** — gross, operating, EBITDA
  - Expansion/contraction expectations
  - Drivers of change
- [ ] **EPS guidance** — GAAP and adjusted
  - Bridge from prior year
  - Impact of buybacks
- [ ] **Free cash flow guidance** — absolute or conversion
- [ ] **Tax rate expectations**

### Capital Allocation (PRIMARY FOCUS)

- [ ] **Capital allocation framework** — ranked priorities
- [ ] **Dividend policy** — payout ratio, growth expectations
- [ ] **Buyback authorization** — current amount, deployment pace
- [ ] **M&A strategy** — criteria, focus areas, deal size range
- [ ] **CapEx plans** — growth vs. maintenance split
- [ ] **Debt management** — leverage targets, refinancing plans
- [ ] **Cash deployment timeline** — when will capital be returned

### Efficiency & Operations

- [ ] **Cost savings programs** — targets, timeline, progress
- [ ] **Productivity initiatives** — automation, restructuring
- [ ] **Working capital optimization** — DSO, DPO, inventory turns
- [ ] **SG&A leverage** — expense growth vs. revenue growth
- [ ] **Margin bridge** — what's driving expansion/contraction

### Returns Framework

- [ ] **ROIC/ROE targets** — and current performance
- [ ] **Hurdle rates** — for capital deployment decisions
- [ ] **TSR targets** — if given
- [ ] **Economic profit** — if company uses this framework

### Balance Sheet

- [ ] **Leverage targets** — Net Debt/EBITDA or similar
- [ ] **Credit rating strategy** — maintain/upgrade
- [ ] **Liquidity position** — cash, credit facilities
- [ ] **Debt maturity profile** — upcoming maturities
- [ ] **Pension/OPEB status** — funding, assumptions

---

## Red Flag Triggers

Watch for and flag if present:

- [ ] Financial targets rely heavily on "macro improvement"
- [ ] Cost savings targets seem aggressive vs. historical achievement
- [ ] Buyback authorization announced but slow deployment history
- [ ] Dividend cut/suspension language (even hedged)
- [ ] Leverage increasing without clear strategic rationale
- [ ] Non-GAAP adjustments growing or changing definition
- [ ] Missing reconciliation of adjusted metrics
- [ ] Tax rate assumptions seem optimistic
- [ ] Working capital improvements that seem unsustainable

---

## Quality Signals

Positive signs to note:

- [ ] Clear capital allocation waterfall/framework
- [ ] Historical delivery against prior CMD targets
- [ ] Conservative assumptions acknowledged
- [ ] Scenario analysis shown (bull/base/bear)
- [ ] Clear hurdle rates for M&A
- [ ] Debt reduction prioritized if leveraged
- [ ] Buyback execution at disciplined prices

## Context Questions

Answer these in the synthesis:

1. Did they deliver on prior CMD commitments?
2. Are targets more or less ambitious vs. prior year?
3. What's the biggest execution risk?
4. Is capital allocation policy shareholder-friendly?
5. Are there hidden dependencies in the guidance (M&A, macro)?
