# Quarterly Presentation — Event Checklist

**Event Characteristics:**
- Released with quarterly earnings
- Focus on recent results and near-term outlook
- Often includes supplemental data slides
- May include earnings call transcript
- High frequency (4x/year)

**Primary Focus:** Results vs. expectations and near-term guidance updates

---

## Mandatory Extraction

### Results vs. Expectations

- [ ] **Revenue** — reported vs. consensus vs. guidance
- [ ] **EPS** — GAAP and adjusted vs. expectations
- [ ] **Segment performance** — which beat/missed
- [ ] **Key operating metrics** — vs. prior quarter and prior year
- [ ] **Notable one-time items** — restructuring, gains/losses

### Guidance Updates

- [ ] **Full-year guidance** — raised/lowered/maintained
  - Revenue
  - EPS
  - Margins
  - FCF
- [ ] **Next quarter guidance** — if given
- [ ] **Any guidance withdrawn** — note and explain why
- [ ] **Changes from prior quarter** — what shifted

### Operational Highlights

- [ ] **Segment trends** — acceleration/deceleration
- [ ] **Geographic trends** — strength/weakness by region
- [ ] **New product/service launches** — revenue impact
- [ ] **Customer wins/losses** — significant contracts
- [ ] **Pricing actions** — increases, mix shifts

### Capital Deployment (This Quarter)

- [ ] **Share repurchases** — amount, shares, avg price
- [ ] **Dividends** — declared, paid
- [ ] **M&A activity** — completed, announced, pipeline
- [ ] **CapEx** — spending vs. plan
- [ ] **Debt actions** — issuance, repayment

### Balance Sheet Changes

- [ ] **Cash position** — change from prior quarter
- [ ] **Debt changes** — new issuance, repayments
- [ ] **Working capital** — notable shifts
- [ ] **Inventory** — build or draw-down

---

## Red Flag Triggers

Watch for and flag if present:

- [ ] Beat on revenue, miss on margin (quality concern)
- [ ] Beat driven by one-time items
- [ ] Guidance raised but qualitative language cautious
- [ ] Guidance lowered with blame on "external factors"
- [ ] Key operating metrics not disclosed (previously were)
- [ ] Non-GAAP adjustments increasing
- [ ] Inventory building faster than revenue
- [ ] DSO/receivables increasing without explanation
- [ ] Management avoiding specific questions in Q&A
- [ ] Change in accounting treatment

---

## Quality Signals

Positive signs to note:

- [ ] Beat and raise (results exceeded expectations + guidance raised)
- [ ] Quality beat (margins expanded, not just revenue)
- [ ] Strong organic growth (not M&A or currency driven)
- [ ] Management addressing challenges directly
- [ ] Detailed segment disclosure
- [ ] Clear explanation of one-time items

---

## Q&A Analysis

If earnings call transcript available:

- [ ] **Analyst tone** — bullish, skeptical, neutral
- [ ] **Key questions** — what are analysts most focused on
- [ ] **Non-answers** — questions management deflected
- [ ] **Forward commentary** — hints about next quarter
- [ ] **Competitive commentary** — mentions of competitors
- [ ] **Management confidence** — qualitative assessment
