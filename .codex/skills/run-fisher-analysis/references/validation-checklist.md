# Validation Checklist: run-fisher-analysis

Use this checklist to verify output completeness before finalizing the Fisher 15-Point Analysis.

---

## SECTION A: Critical Validation (Check First)

These items MUST pass before the analysis is complete. If any fail, fix before outputting.

- [ ] **All 15 points scored** — Summary Scorecard table has all 15 rows with valid scores
- [ ] **No score without evidence** — Every score in Detailed Analysis cites specific facts (not vague assertions)
- [ ] **Points 14 & 15 disqualifier check done** — Both reviewed for -2; if either is -2, explicit warning in verdict
- [ ] **Total score math correct** — Sum of 15 individual scores equals stated Total Score
- [ ] **Verdict matches score range** — See interpretation table below

### Score Interpretation Table

| Total Score | Interpretation | Expected Verdict |
|-------------|----------------|------------------|
| +20 to +30 | Exceptional quality; rare | PASS |
| +10 to +19 | High quality; strong candidate | PASS |
| 0 to +9 | Average to above-average; needs other factors | MARGINAL |
| -10 to -1 | Below average; significant concerns | MARGINAL or FAIL |
| -30 to -11 | Poor quality; likely avoid | FAIL |

**Disqualifier Override:** A -2 on Point 14 (Transparency) or Point 15 (Integrity) can convert PASS to MARGINAL or FAIL regardless of total score. Fisher's quote must be referenced: "If there is a serious question of the lack of a strong management sense of trusteeship for stockholders, the investor should never seriously consider participating in such an enterprise."

## SECTION D: Evidence Standards

### Quantified Evidence Required

- [ ] **Numbers over adjectives** — "Operating margin 35% vs. industry 20%" not "strong margins"
- [ ] **Specific dates/timeframes** — "Revenue CAGR 12% over 2019-2024" not "strong growth"
- [ ] **Comparisons to peers** — Context showing above/below average
- [ ] **Source citations** — Prior research files named, external sources noted

### Evidence Type by Point

| Point | Evidence Type Required |
|-------|----------------------|
| 1 (Market Potential) | TAM size, growth rate, market share, expansion vectors |
| 2 (Product Development) | R&D spend trend, new product launches, pipeline |
| 3 (R&D Effectiveness) | R&D as % revenue, successful products from R&D |
| 4 (Sales Organization) | Revenue per salesperson, CAC, win rates, turnover |
| 5 (Profit Margins) | Gross/operating/net margins vs. industry and over time |
| 6 (Margin Improvement) | Specific initiatives, pricing trends, cost program results |
| 7 (Labor Relations) | Glassdoor rating, turnover rate, labor history |
| 8 (Executive Relations) | C-suite tenure, turnover, compensation structure |
| 9 (Management Depth) | Succession plans, internal promotions, departures handled |
| 10 (Cost Controls) | Forecast accuracy, restatement history, audit issues |
| 11 (Industry Clues) | Industry-specific KPIs vs. competitors |
| 12 (Long-Range Outlook) | Capex/R&D trends, management incentive horizons |
| 13 (Equity Dilution) | Share count trend, SBC as % revenue, FCF vs. capex needs |
| 14 (Transparency) | Earnings call behavior in good/bad times, disclosure quality |
| 15 (Integrity) | Ethics history, related party transactions, SEC issues |

### Zero Score Justification

- [ ] **No "0" cop-outs** — Every 0 score has evidence showing genuinely average
- [ ] **"0" means average with proof** — Not "I couldn't find information" or "not sure"

## SECTION E: Disqualifier Check

### Points 14 & 15 Deep Review

- [ ] **Point 14 (Transparency) reviewed** for:
  - Communication quality during difficult periods
  - Willingness to discuss challenges and mistakes
  - Treatment of bad news vs. good news

- [ ] **Point 15 (Integrity) reviewed** for:
  - History of ethical issues or scandals
  - Treatment of minority shareholders
  - Related party transactions
  - Regulatory/legal problems

### If Either Point Scores -2

- [ ] **Explicit warning in Verdict section** — Cannot be buried in detailed analysis
- [ ] **Fisher's quote referenced** — "If there is a serious question of the lack of a strong management sense of trusteeship for stockholders, the investor should never seriously consider participating in such an enterprise."
- [ ] **Verdict adjusted appropriately** — Even high total score should be MARGINAL or FAIL

## Common Failure Modes

| Issue | Check | How to Fix |
|-------|-------|-----------|
| Missing point | Scorecard has <15 rows | Add missing point(s) with full analysis |
| Score without evidence | "[Company] has good margins" | Change to "Operating margin 35% vs. industry 20%" |
| Vague analysis | "Management seems capable" | Add tenure, track record, specific achievements |
| Wrong total | Sum ≠ stated Total Score | Recalculate and fix |
| Verdict mismatch | Score +15 but Verdict is FAIL | Align verdict with interpretation table |
| Skipped disqualifier check | No mention of Points 14/15 severity | Explicitly review both for -2 status |
| "0" without justification | "0 — nothing notable" | Add evidence showing genuinely average |
| Missing sources | No files listed | Add all prior research files used |
| R&D marked N/A without alternative | Just "N/A" for Point 3 | Evaluate equivalent innovation capability |
| Disqualifier buried | -2 on Point 15 mentioned only in scorecard | Add explicit warning to Verdict section |

## Validation Examples

### Evidence Quality

| Format | Pass/Fail | Reason |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| "Operating margin 35% vs. industry average 20% (per 10-K Synthesis)" | PASS | Quantified, compared, sourced |
| "Margins are strong" | FAIL | No numbers, no comparison |
| "Glassdoor 4.2/5, CEO approval 85%, 8% turnover (Scuttlebutt memo)" | PASS | Multiple metrics, sourced |
| "Employees seem satisfied" | FAIL | No evidence |
| "R&D at 8% of revenue has produced 5 major product launches in 3 years (10-K)" | PASS | Specific, quantified, sourced |
| "Good R&D track record" | FAIL | Vague |
| "0 — industry-average margins at 15% vs. sector median 14.5%" | PASS | Zero justified with evidence |
| "0 — nothing notable here" | FAIL | No evidence for "average" claim |

### Disqualifier Handling

| Scenario | Pass/Fail | Reason |
|----------|-----------|--------|
| Point 15 = -2, Total = +18, Verdict = MARGINAL with warning | PASS | Disqualifier properly affects verdict |
| Point 15 = -2, Total = +18, Verdict = PASS (no warning) | FAIL | Disqualifier ignored |
| Point 14 = -2, Fisher quote included in Verdict section | PASS | Proper handling |
| Point 14 = -2, Fisher quote missing | FAIL | Missing required warning |
