### What to Assess

#### 1. Moat & Competitive Advantage (Full Assessment)

Evaluate:
- **Moat Type:** Identify which moat types apply (brand, network effects, cost advantages, switching costs, scale, intangibles). Rate each as Primary/Secondary/None

- **Customer Perspective:** Why they choose, what would make them switch (from Scuttlebutt)

**Score:** -2 to +2

**Remember the +2 Gate:** WIDE & WIDENING requires multiple reinforcing moats, no erosion signals, and no emerging threats. Single-source moat or any competitive concern caps at +1.

## Scoring Reference

### Moat Scoring

| Score | Assessment | Criteria |
|-------|------------|----------|
| +2 | WIDE & WIDENING | Multiple reinforcing moats, ROIC >15% sustained, no erosion, no threats. NO concerns. |
| +1 | WIDE & STABLE | Strong moat but single-source OR facing emerging threat OR stable not widening |
| 0 | NARROW | Minor competitive advantage, easily replicable, or early erosion signals |
| -1 | ERODING | Clear evidence of moat deterioration, market share loss, pricing pressure |
| -2 | NONE | No competitive advantage. Commodity business competing on price |

### Predictability Scoring

| Score | Assessment | Criteria |
|-------|------------|----------|
| +2 | HIGH | Both short-term AND long-term highly predictable. Rare. |
| +1 | MODERATE-HIGH | Long-term predictable, short-term may vary with cycles/events |
| 0 | MODERATE | Some predictability but notable uncertainty factors |
| -1 | LOW | Significant unpredictability in outcomes |
| -2 | VERY LOW | Business outcomes highly uncertain. Speculation required. |

### Long-Term Orientation Scoring

| Score | Assessment | Criteria |
|-------|------------|----------|
| +2 | STRONG COMPOUNDER | Clear tailwinds, high ROIC × reinvestment, comfortable 20-year hold |
| +1 | MODERATE | Net positive long-term, but headwinds or limited reinvestment opportunity |
| 0 | LIMITED | Neutral long-term outlook, tailwinds roughly balance headwinds |
| -1 | UNLIKELY | More headwinds than tailwinds, long-term concerns |
| -2 | VALUE TRAP | Looks cheap but structural decline likely |

### Lollapalooza Scoring

| Score | Assessment | Criteria |
|-------|------------|----------|
| +2 | PRESENT | Multiple forces combine and reinforce. Clear virtuous cycle. Rare. |
| +1 | PARTIAL | Some combining effects but not extraordinary |
| 0 | NONE | Advantages exist but don't compound together. Most companies. |

## Key Principles

1. **Synthesize across sources:** This stage has the most input documents — find patterns and contradictions
2. **Validate management claims:** Does external evidence (Scuttlebutt, competitors) match what management says?
3. **Moat is central:** Munger focuses heavily on durable competitive advantage — be rigorous here
4. **Quantify where possible:** "Strong moat" is weaker than "10-point combined ratio advantage = $2B annual excess profit"
5. **Finalize partial assessments:** Predictability and Long-Term Orientation must be complete after this stage
6. **Prepare for synthesis:** Stage 4 will only read Working Analysis v3 — make it comprehensive
