## Required Quirks (Section 3)

### 1. Revenue Classification (Royalties vs Company-Operated)

- **What:** Royalty revenue (4-6% of franchisee sales) vs. company-operated restaurant sales (100% of sales)
- **Distortion:** Company-operated sales look like high revenue but have much lower margins (~15-20% vs. >60%)
- **Adjustment:** Analyze royalty/franchise revenue separately; understand franchised vs. company-op mix trends

### 2. Refranchising Gains

- **What:** Selling company-operated restaurants to franchisees generates one-time gains
- **Distortion:** Refranchising gains flow through income, inflating earnings during asset-light transition
- **Adjustment:** Strip refranchising gains from core earnings; track as separate activity

### 3. Lease Obligations (Master Lease Structures)

- **What:** Many QSRs sublease real estate to franchisees, creating off-balance sheet obligations
- **Distortion:** Lease obligations can be massive; rental income and expense largely offset but create risk
- **Adjustment:** Track net rental income; understand lease liability exposure; compare to peers with different structures

### 4. Advertising Fund Accounting

- **What:** Advertising contributions collected from franchisees; spent on system marketing
- **Distortion:** Revenue and expense recognized for pass-through funds; inflates both revenue and cost
- **Adjustment:** Focus on "revenue excluding advertising funds" for franchise margin analysis

## Valuation Guidance (Section 8)

### Primary Valuation Method

**EV/EBITDA with franchise % context**

| Franchise % | Justified EV/EBITDA | Rationale |
|-------------|---------------------|-----------|
| >95% franchised, strong comps | 20-28x | Asset-light premium |
| 80-95% franchised | 16-22x | Mixed model |
| <80% franchised | 12-18x | More company-operated exposure |

### Secondary Methods

- **FCF Yield:** 3-5% for quality; high cash conversion
- **P/E:** 22-30x for premium; stable earnings stream
- **Dividend Yield:** Many QSRs have high payout; 2-3% typical

### Methods to AVOID

| Method | Why Inappropriate |
|--------|-------------------|
| EV/Revenue | Franchise vs. company-op revenue not comparable |
| Comparison to company-operated peers | Completely different model |
| EBITDA without lease adjustment | Operating leases material |
| Raw margin comparisons | Mix distorts significantly |

### DCF Considerations

- **Comp sales assumptions:** 2-4% long-term for mature
- **Unit growth:** International expansion often key
- **Royalty rate stability:** Rarely changes
- **Lease obligations:** Model as debt-like

### Owner's Earnings Adjustments

```
Operating Income                              $X,XXXM (FYxxxx)
```

## Peer Selection (Section 7)

### Match On

| Factor | Why It Matters |
|--------|----------------|
| Franchise % mix | Asset-light vs. company-operated |
| Daypart (breakfast/lunch/dinner) | Different competitive dynamics |
| Price point (QSR vs. fast casual) | Different economics |
| Geographic footprint | Maturity and growth runway |
| Real estate ownership structure | Lease vs. own economics |

### Flag as False Comparables

| Company Type | Why Not Comparable |
|--------------|-------------------|
| Company-operated restaurants | Different margin structure |
| Casual dining (sit-down) | Different daypart and labor model |
| Coffee/beverage focused | Different transaction sizes |
| Convenience stores | Different traffic patterns |
| Food distributors | Different position in chain |

## Red Flags to Document

| Red Flag | What It Signals |
|----------|-----------------|
| Negative comp sales for 2+ quarters | Demand issues |
| Transaction declines despite check growth | Volume weakness |
| Net unit closures | Franchisee economics challenged |
| Franchisee complaints public | System health issues |
| Royalty rate pressure | Franchisee profitability concerns |
| Labor cost increases flowing to franchisees | Margin pressure in system |

---

## Green Flags to Document

| Green Flag | What It Signals |
|------------|-----------------|
| Positive comp sales and transactions | Healthy demand |
| Strong unit development pipeline | Franchisee confidence |
| International expansion success | Long growth runway |
| Digital/delivery growing % | Modern consumer engagement |
| Franchise % >95% | Asset-light model |
