## Required Metrics (Section 4)

### Backlog & Revenue Metrics (ALL REQUIRED)

| Metric | Benchmark | Why It Matters |
|--------|-----------|----------------|
| Backlog Coverage (Backlog/Annual Revenue) | 2-3x strong; <1.5x concerning | Revenue visibility |
| Book-to-Bill Ratio | >1.0x strong; <0.95x for 2+ qtrs concerning | Order momentum |
| Backlog Mix (funded vs. unfunded) | Track funded % and conversion timing | Near-term revenue certainty |
| Organic Revenue Growth | >3% strong; flat to negative concerning | Core business trajectory |

### Contract & Margin Metrics (ALL REQUIRED)

| Metric | Benchmark | Why It Matters |
|--------|-----------|----------------|
| Fixed-Price % of Revenue | 40-60% typical; >70% execution risk | Margin risk profile |
| Operating Margin | >10% strong; 8-10% adequate; <8% concerning | Execution efficiency |
| Segment Operating Margin Trends | Watch for compression | Program performance |
| EAC Adjustment Impact | Track positive vs. negative | Management credibility |

### Customer & Concentration Metrics (ALL REQUIRED)

| Metric | Benchmark | Why It Matters |
|--------|-----------|----------------|
| USG Concentration | 60-80% normal; >90% policy risk | Customer diversification |
| International/FMS % | 20-35% good diversification | Revenue diversity |
| Top 3 Program Concentration | <40% healthy; single program >30% risky | Program risk |
| R&D / Revenue | 2-5% typical (much funded by customer) | Investment profile |

## Required Quirks (Section 3)

### 1. Percentage-of-Completion Revenue Recognition (ASC 606)

- **What:** Revenue recognized as costs incurred / estimated total costs × contract value
- **Distortion:** Revenue and margin front-loaded or back-loaded based on cost estimates; EACs drive earnings
- **Adjustment:** Track EAC adjustment history; positive adjustments = conservative initial estimates; negative = optimistic

### 2. Estimate-at-Completion (EAC) Changes

- **What:** Management updates total expected cost and revenue; changes flow through current period
- **Distortion:** Earnings can swing significantly on EAC adjustments; management judgment dominant
- **Adjustment:** Disclose EAC impact on margins; track positive vs. negative adjustments over time

### 3. Contract Assets vs Accounts Receivable

- **What:** Contract assets = revenue recognized but not yet billed (work performed ahead of billing milestones)
- **Distortion:** Balance sheet shows large contract assets that haven't converted to cash
- **Adjustment:** Track contract asset turnover; rising contract assets may signal billing issues or revenue acceleration

### 4. Progress Payments and Negative Working Capital

- **What:** Government customers provide progress payments before work complete; creates customer financing
- **Distortion:** Negative working capital looks unusual but is actually favorable (interest-free financing)
- **Adjustment:** Understand cash conversion cycle; progress payments fund operations

## Valuation Guidance (Section 8)

### Primary Valuation Method

**EV/EBITDA with backlog context**

| Business Mix | Justified EV/EBITDA | Rationale |
|--------------|---------------------|-----------|
| Services-heavy (higher margin) | 12-16x | More predictable, less capital intensive |
| Platform/products-heavy | 9-13x | More lumpy, higher capex |
| Turnaround/restructuring | 7-10x | Uncertainty premium |

### Secondary Methods

- **DCF with backlog modeling:** Convert backlog to revenue with margin assumptions
- **P/E:** 14-20x for quality; normalize for EAC adjustments
- **Sum-of-Parts:** When divisions have different profiles

### Methods to AVOID

| Method | Why Inappropriate |
|--------|-------------------|
| Raw P/E | EAC adjustments distort earnings |
| Revenue multiples | Contract mix drives margins |
| P/B | Pension and contract accounting distort book |
| FCF without pension adjustment | Pension contributions distort cash flow |

### DCF Considerations

- **Backlog conversion:** Model multi-year revenue from current backlog
- **Book-to-bill trajectory:** New awards sustain revenue
- **Margin realization:** Fixed-price vs. cost-plus mix
- **Pension obligations:** Material cash flow impact; track funded status

### Owner's Earnings Adjustments

```
Net Income                                    $X,XXXM (FYxxxx)
+ D&A                                          $X,XXXM [per 10-K]
- Maintenance CapEx                           ($XXXM) [EST: 60-70% of total capex]
+ Pension Expense (non-cash FAS)               $XXXM [add back non-cash]
- Pension Contributions (cash CAS)            ($XXXM) [deduct actual cash]
+/- EAC Adjustment Normalization                $XXXM [if unusual positive/negative]
= Owner's Earnings                           ~$X,XXXM

Note: FAS vs. CAS pension accounting creates recurring GAAP vs. cash differences
```

## Peer Selection (Section 7)

### Match On

| Factor | Why It Matters |
|--------|----------------|
| Contract mix (FP vs. cost-plus) | Risk and margin profile |
| Business mix (platforms vs. services) | Capital intensity and cyclicality |
| Customer mix (DoD vs. intel vs. commercial) | Budget dynamics |
| Technology focus (air/land/sea/space/cyber) | End-market exposure |
| Size and prime vs. sub status | Contract competition dynamics |

### Flag as False Comparables

| Company Type | Why Not Comparable |
|--------------|-------------------|
| Commercial aerospace (Boeing, Airbus) | Different customer, cycle, economics |
| Government IT services (pure) | Different margin profile |
| Defense sub-contractors | Different scale and contract terms |
| European defense (EADS, BAE) | Different customer and regulatory |
| Space companies (SpaceX) | Different growth and risk profile |

## Red Flags to Document

| Red Flag | What It Signals |
|----------|-----------------|
| Book-to-bill <1.0x for 3+ quarters | Award drought |
| Negative EAC adjustments recurring | Optimistic bidding or execution issues |
| Single program >25% of revenue | Concentration risk |
| Margins declining without mix shift | Pricing pressure or cost overruns |
| Fixed-price program overruns | Execution capability concerns |
| International awards slowing | FMS pipeline weakening |

---

## Green Flags to Document

| Green Flag | What It Signals |
|------------|-----------------|
| Backlog >3x revenue | Multi-year visibility |
| Consistent positive EAC adjustments | Conservative estimating |
| Position on next-gen platforms | Future relevance |
| Growing services mix | Higher margins, more recurring |
| International expansion | Revenue diversification |
