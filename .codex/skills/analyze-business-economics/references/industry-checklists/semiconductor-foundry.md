## Required Quirks (Section 3)

### 1. Depreciation-Driven Cost Structure

- **What:** Semiconductor equipment depreciated over 5-7 years; D&A represents ~40% of cost of goods sold
- **Distortion:** Reported earnings highly sensitive to depreciation policy; new fabs depress margins for years
- **Adjustment:** Analyze gross margin trends by node; understand that new capacity drag is temporary

### 2. Growth vs Maintenance Capex

- **What:** Capex includes both new fab construction (growth) and equipment upgrades (maintenance)
- **Distortion:** All capex treated equally, but 70-80% is typically growth investment during expansion phases
- **Adjustment:** Management guidance distinguishes growth vs. maintenance; use maintenance capex for Owner's Earnings

### 3. Foreign Currency Effects

- **What:** Revenue largely in USD; costs in multiple currencies (TWD, JPY, EUR)
- **Distortion:** Currency movements create margin swings independent of operations
- **Adjustment:** Track constant currency growth and margins when available; understand USD strength impact

### 4. Government Subsidies (CHIPS Act, etc.)

- **What:** Governments provide billions in subsidies, tax credits, and incentives for fab construction
- **Distortion:** Capex and depreciation reduced; margins appear higher; creates geographic distortions
- **Adjustment:** Track net capex after subsidies; understand subsidy timing and conditions

## Valuation Guidance (Section 8)

### Primary Valuation Method

**EV/EBITDA through the cycle**

| Market Position | Justified EV/EBITDA | Rationale |
|-----------------|---------------------|-----------|
| Technology leader (3nm+) | 15-22x | Premium for irreplaceable position |
| Competitive (7nm capable) | 10-15x | Solid but not leading |
| Lagging nodes | 6-10x | Commodity-like competition |

### Secondary Methods

- **P/E Normalized:** Use mid-cycle margins; avoid peak or trough
- **EV/Wafer Capacity:** For relative valuation across competitors
- **DCF:** With explicit capex cycle modeling

### Methods to AVOID

| Method | Why Inappropriate |
|--------|-------------------|
| P/B | Heavily impacted by depreciation policy differences |
| Reported FCF | Negative during expansion; not indicative of earning power |
| P/E at peak margins | Cyclicality creates misleading valuations |
| Simple Revenue Multiple | Ignores massive margin differences by node |

### DCF Considerations

- **Normalize capex:** Use maintenance capex (25-30% of expansion capex) for steady state
- **Model technology transitions:** Each new node requires step-up investment
- **Customer concentration:** Top 3 customers often >50% of revenue
- **Geopolitical risk:** Taiwan exposure, trade restrictions

### Owner's Earnings Adjustments

```
Net Income                                    $X,XXXM (FYxxxx)
+ Depreciation & Amortization                 $XX,XXXM [per 10-K]
- Maintenance CapEx                           ($XX,XXXM) [EST: 20-30% of total capex]
- Growth CapEx treated as expense             ($XXM) [Note: Actually asset building]
+/- Currency Normalization                       $XXXM [if material FX impact]
= Owner's Earnings                           ~$XX,XXXM

Note: During expansion phases, reported FCF understates true earning power
      During downturns, FCF overstates due to delayed capex
```

## Peer Selection (Section 7)

### Match On

| Factor | Why It Matters |
|--------|----------------|
| Technology node capability | Defines competitive positioning |
| Customer concentration | Revenue stability |
| Geographic footprint | Geopolitical and supply chain risk |
| Business model (pure-play vs. IDM) | Different economics |
| Capacity size and utilization | Scale effects |

### Flag as False Comparables

| Company Type | Why Not Comparable |
|--------------|-------------------|
| IDMs (Intel, Samsung) | Different business model; internal captive demand |
| Fabless companies (Nvidia, AMD) | Asset-light design vs. capital-intensive manufacturing |
| Equipment suppliers (ASML, Applied) | Upstream supplier, different economics |
| Memory foundries (Micron) | Commodity memory vs. logic foundry |
| OSAT (packaging/test) | Downstream service, different margins |

## Red Flags to Document

| Red Flag | What It Signals |
|----------|-----------------|
| Advanced node % declining | Technology leadership slipping |
| Gross margin compression >3pts YoY | Pricing pressure or cost issues |
| Utilization below 70% for 2+ quarters | Demand weakness |
| Single customer >30% revenue | Concentration risk |
| Capex delays or cuts during expected expansion | Confidence or financing issues |
| Technology roadmap slippage | Execution problems |

---

## Green Flags to Document

| Green Flag | What It Signals |
|------------|-----------------|
| Advanced node revenue growth >20% | Technology leadership paying off |
| Gross margin expansion with new nodes | Pricing power improving |
| Capacity sold out 12+ months forward | Strong demand visibility |
| Diversified customer base across end markets | Reduced cyclicality |
| Successful N+1 node ramp | Continued technology leadership |
