## Required Quirks (Section 3)

### 1. GAAP Depreciation vs Economic Reality

- **What:** Towers depreciated over 15-20 years, but well-maintained towers last 40+ years
- **Distortion:** GAAP net income massively understates economic earnings; D&A overstated vs. actual decline
- **Adjustment:** Use AFFO (adds back depreciation, deducts maintenance capex); never value on GAAP earnings

### 2. Straight-Line Revenue Recognition

- **What:** Lease escalators recognized evenly over lease term rather than as cash received
- **Distortion:** Early years show higher revenue than cash; later years show lower revenue than cash
- **Adjustment:** Track cash revenue vs. straight-line revenue; understand working capital timing

### 3. REIT Distribution Requirements

- **What:** Must distribute 90% of taxable income; creates forced high payout regardless of opportunities
- **Distortion:** High dividend yield may signal distribution requirement rather than shareholder alignment
- **Adjustment:** Focus on AFFO payout ratio (vs. taxable income); assess discretionary cash after dividends

### 4. Acquisition Accounting

- **What:** Tower portfolio acquisitions create significant goodwill and intangibles
- **Distortion:** Balance sheet carries acquisition premiums; amortization flows through income
- **Adjustment:** Focus on cash returns on invested capital; track acquisition performance vs. underwriting

## Valuation Guidance (Section 8)

### Primary Valuation Method

**Price-to-AFFO**

| Growth Profile | Justified P/AFFO | Rationale |
|----------------|------------------|-----------|
| >5% AFFO growth, strong carrier activity | 22-28x | Premium for growth and stability |
| 3-5% AFFO growth | 18-22x | Moderate growth platform |
| <3% AFFO growth | 14-18x | Mature, yield-focused |

### Secondary Methods

- **EV/EBITDA:** 18-25x for quality towers; useful for M&A context
- **Dividend Yield:** 2.5-4% yield typical for growth; 4-5% for mature
- **Cap Rate on NOI:** Compare to real estate alternatives

### Methods to AVOID

| Method | Why Inappropriate |
|--------|-------------------|
| P/E | GAAP earnings distorted by depreciation |
| P/B | Book value reflects acquisition accounting, not asset value |
| FFO (traditional) | Use AFFO which adjusts for maintenance capex |
| Reported Net Income | Essentially meaningless for REITs |

### DCF Considerations

- **Escalator modeling:** Built-in 3-5% annual growth
- **Churn assumptions:** 1.5-2.5% annual typical
- **5G/network densification:** Additional growth driver
- **Maintenance capex:** 2-3% of revenue typical

### Owner's Earnings Adjustments

```
FFO (Funds From Operations)                   $X,XXXM (FYxxxx)
- Maintenance CapEx                           ($XXXM) [typically 2-3% revenue]
- Straight-Line Revenue Adjustment               ($XXXM) [if material]
+ Straight-Line Expense Adjustment               $XXXM [ground lease timing]
= AFFO (Adjusted FFO)                        ~$X,XXXM

Note: AFFO is the standard "Owner's Earnings" for tower REITs
```

## Peer Selection (Section 7)

### Match On

| Factor | Why It Matters |
|--------|----------------|
| Asset type (towers vs. small cells vs. fiber) | Different economics |
| Geographic footprint (US vs. international) | Currency and growth profiles |
| Tenant concentration | Revenue risk |
| Leverage profile | Risk and growth capacity |
| Portfolio maturity | Acquisition accounting impact |

### Flag as False Comparables

| Company Type | Why Not Comparable |
|--------------|-------------------|
| Data center REITs | Different tenant dynamics, power costs |
| Traditional real estate REITs | Different lease structures, asset lives |
| Fiber/connectivity companies | Infrastructure but different economics |
| Wireless carriers | Customers, not peers |
| Equipment companies (Ericsson) | Vendor relationship, not landlord |

## Red Flags to Document

| Red Flag | What It Signals |
|----------|-----------------|
| Churn rate >3% sustained | Carrier consolidation or technology shift |
| AFFO payout ratio >90% | Limited reinvestment capacity |
| Net debt/EBITDA >7x | Overleveraged for interest rate moves |
| Organic growth <2% | Market saturation or pricing pressure |
| Single tenant >50% revenue | Concentration risk |
| 5G deployment slower than expected | Growth thesis weakening |

---

## Green Flags to Document

| Green Flag | What It Signals |
|------------|-----------------|
| Co-location/amendment activity strong | Revenue per tower expanding |
| Long-weighted average lease term | Revenue visibility |
| AFFO payout ratio <75% | Reinvestment capacity |
| Carrier 5G spending acceleration | Industry tailwind |
| Small cell/fiber diversification | Multiple growth vectors |
