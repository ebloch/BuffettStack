## Required Metrics (Section 4)

### Fleet & Portfolio Metrics (ALL REQUIRED)

| Metric | Benchmark | Why It Matters |
|--------|-----------|----------------|
| Book Value Per Share Growth | >10% CAGR strong; 5-10% adequate; <5% concerning | Primary value creation metric for lessors |
| Gain-on-Sale Margin | 20-25% strong; 15-20% healthy; <10% concerning | Validates NBV is conservative vs. market |
| Fleet Utilization Rate | >97% excellent; 95-97% good; <93% red flag | Capacity to generate revenue from assets |
| Adjusted Debt-to-Equity | <2.5x excellent; 2.5-2.7x acceptable; >3.0x concerning | Leverage relative to asset-backed nature |
| Average Fleet Age | <7 years excellent; 7-8 years good; >10 years concerning | Technology risk and residual value exposure |
| Net Lease Yield | 10-11% strong; 9-10% acceptable; <8% concerning | Returns on deployed capital |
| New Technology Fleet % | >75% excellent; 70-75% good; <60% concerning | Competitive positioning and fuel efficiency |

### Cash Flow Metrics (ALL REQUIRED)

| Metric | Benchmark | Why It Matters |
|--------|-----------|----------------|
| Cash Flow from Operations | Watch for maintenance reserve distortion | Not true cash generation |
| Maintenance Reserve Receipts | Track separately from core CFO | Liability, not income |
| Aircraft Sales Volume | 10-15% of fleet annually typical | Portfolio management activity |

## Required Quirks (Section 3)

### 1. Depreciation vs Economic Reality

- **What:** Aircraft depreciated over 25-year life to 15% residual, but well-maintained aircraft retain 40-50% of original value
- **Distortion:** Net Book Value (NBV) systematically understates market value by 20-40% for newer aircraft
- **Adjustment:** Track gain-on-sale margins over time; consistent 20%+ margins validate conservative book values

### 2. Maintenance Reserve Accounting

- **What:** Lessees pay monthly maintenance reserves that lessor receives as cash but books as liability
- **Distortion:** CFO appears higher than economic reality; reserves released when maintenance performed
- **Adjustment:** Distinguish maintenance reserve receipts from core operating cash flow; watch for net position changes

### 3. Trading Gains Volatility

- **What:** Aircraft sales generate gains/losses that flow through income statement
- **Distortion:** EPS can swing 30%+ based on timing and volume of sales; masks underlying lease economics
- **Adjustment:** Analyze lease revenue and fleet economics separately from trading activity; normalize for sales volume

### 4. Interest Rate Mismatch

- **What:** Lease rates largely fixed at origination; debt can be floating or fixed at different terms
- **Distortion:** Rising rates compress spreads immediately while lease rates adjust slowly over years
- **Adjustment:** Track interest coverage ratio trends; model sensitivity to rate changes

## Valuation Guidance (Section 8)

### Primary Valuation Method

**Price-to-Book (P/B) with gain margin validation**

| BVPS Growth + ROE | Justified P/B | Rationale |
|-------------------|---------------|-----------|
| >12% BVPS growth, consistent gains | 1.2-1.5x | Premium for capital allocation |
| 8-12% BVPS growth | 1.0-1.2x | Fair value for solid compounder |
| <8% BVPS growth | 0.8-1.0x | Discount for weak returns |

### Secondary Methods

- **Sum-of-Parts:** Fleet NBV + present value of lease contracts + trading operation value
- **P/E on Adjusted Earnings:** Normalize for trading gains, use 6-10x range

### Methods to AVOID

| Method | Why Inappropriate |
|--------|-------------------|
| EV/EBITDA | D&A is massive but not economic; EBITDA meaningless |
| Reported FCF | Maintenance reserves distort; growth capex mixed with replacement |
| Raw P/E | Trading gains create volatility |
| EV/Revenue | Ignores critical leverage and cost structure |

### Owner's Earnings Adjustments

```
Net Income                                    $X,XXXM (FYxxxx)
- Gain on Sale (normalize to average)         ($XXXM) [if above average]
+ Loss on Sale (add back if below average)       $XXXM
- Maintenance Reserves Net Change             ($XXXM) [if receipts > payments]
+/- Interest Rate Normalization                  $XXXM [if rates abnormal]
= Normalized Owner's Earnings                ~$X,XXXM

Note: For aircraft lessors, focus on BVPS growth as primary value metric
```

## Peer Selection (Section 7)

### Match On

| Factor | Why It Matters |
|--------|----------------|
| Fleet composition (narrowbody/widebody mix) | Different risk/return profiles |
| Geographic customer concentration | Regional vs. global lessors |
| Average fleet age and technology mix | Asset quality drives returns |
| Leverage profile | Balance sheet risk |
| Management team experience | Critical for aviation cycles |

### Flag as False Comparables

| Company Type | Why Not Comparable |
|--------------|-------------------|
| Engine lessors | Different asset dynamics, maintenance cycles |
| Aviation finance companies | Lending vs. residual ownership |
| Airlines | Operating company vs. asset owner |
| Equipment lessors (non-aviation) | Different asset lives, values, markets |

## Red Flags to Document

| Red Flag | What It Signals |
|----------|-----------------|
| Gain margins declining below 15% | NBV may be overstated vs. market |
| Fleet utilization <95% for 2+ quarters | Demand issues or credit quality |
| Average fleet age rising rapidly | Under-investment or weak placement |
| Debt-to-equity >3.0x | Over-leveraged for cycle downturn |
| Concentrated customer exposure >15% | Credit risk concentration |
| New tech fleet % declining | Competitive disadvantage emerging |

---

## Green Flags to Document

| Green Flag | What It Signals |
|------------|-----------------|
| Consistent gain-on-sale margins >20% | Conservative book values |
| BVPS CAGR >10% over 10 years | Excellent capital allocation |
| Order book for new tech aircraft | Securing future fleet quality |
| Global customer diversification | Reduced regional risk |
| Counter-cyclical acquisition capability | Balance sheet strength |
