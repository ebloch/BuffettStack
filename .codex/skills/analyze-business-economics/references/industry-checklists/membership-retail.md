## Required Quirks (Section 3)

### 1. Membership Revenue Recognition

- **What:** Membership fees received upfront but recognized ratably over 12-month period
- **Distortion:** Deferred revenue builds on balance sheet; cash received before revenue recognized
- **Adjustment:** Track membership revenue trend separately; understand timing vs. economic value

### 2. LIFO vs FIFO Inventory Accounting

- **What:** LIFO creates LIFO reserve that grows in inflationary periods
- **Distortion:** LIFO reduces current earnings vs. FIFO; inventory understated on balance sheet
- **Adjustment:** Add LIFO reserve to inventory and equity for fair comparison; note impact on gross margin

### 3. Real Estate Ownership vs Lease

- **What:** Significant owned real estate; different from leased competitors
- **Distortion:** Owned real estate creates higher D&A; hidden value in land appreciation
- **Adjustment:** Track owned vs. leased locations; understand embedded real estate value

### 4. E-commerce Margins

- **What:** E-commerce has different economics (lower margin, higher fulfillment cost)
- **Distortion:** Growing e-commerce mix may compress margins
- **Adjustment:** Track e-commerce penetration and profitability separately

## Valuation Guidance (Section 8)

### Primary Valuation Method

**P/E on Core Earnings**

| Member Growth + Renewal | Justified P/E | Rationale |
|-------------------------|---------------|-----------|
| >3% member growth, >92% renewal | 35-45x | Premium for durability |
| 1-3% member growth, >90% renewal | 28-35x | Quality compounder |
| <1% member growth | 22-28x | Maturing |

### Secondary Methods

- **EV/EBITDA:** 18-25x for premium; less useful given owned real estate
- **FCF Yield:** 2-3% typical for quality; cash generative
- **Sum-of-Parts:** Operating business + real estate value

### Methods to AVOID

| Method | Why Inappropriate |
|--------|-------------------|
| Gross Margin Comparisons to Traditional Retail | Completely different model |
| Revenue Multiple | Low margin makes misleading |
| P/B | LIFO and real estate distort |
| Same metrics as grocery | Different business model |

### DCF Considerations

- **Membership fee growth:** Track member count × fee pricing
- **Same-store sales assumptions:** 3-5% long-term reasonable
- **Gross margin stability:** Model flat to slightly declining
- **Real estate value:** Consider as separate asset

## Peer Selection (Section 7)

### Match On

| Factor | Why It Matters |
|--------|----------------|
| Business model (membership-based) | Core economic driver |
| Geographic footprint | Market maturity |
| Merchandise mix | Category economics |
| Real estate ownership | Balance sheet structure |

### Flag as False Comparables

| Company Type | Why Not Comparable |
|--------------|-------------------|
| Traditional supermarkets | Different model, higher margin |
| Discount retailers (Dollar stores) | Different value proposition |
| Mass merchants (Target, Walmart) | Different member economics |
| Online-only retailers | Different cost structure |
| Specialty retailers | Different category focus |

## Red Flags to Document

| Red Flag | What It Signals |
|----------|-----------------|
| Membership renewal rate declining | Member satisfaction issues |
| Traffic declining for 2+ quarters | Competitive pressure |
| Gross margin expanding materially | Pricing discipline eroding value prop |
| Inventory turnover declining | Demand or assortment issues |
| Member growth stalling in core markets | Saturation |
| E-commerce losses widening | Channel profitability concerns |

---

## Green Flags to Document

| Green Flag | What It Signals |
|------------|-----------------|
| Renewal rate >91% | Strong member loyalty |
| Executive member % growing | Trading up in membership |
| International expansion success | Long growth runway |
| Membership fee increase without churn | Pricing power |
| Traffic and ticket both positive | Healthy demand |
