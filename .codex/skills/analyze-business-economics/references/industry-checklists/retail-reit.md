# Retail REIT — Business Economics Checklist

**Trigger Keywords:** "retail REIT", "triple net", "net lease REIT", "shopping center", "net lease"

---

## Required Metrics (Section 4)

### Occupancy & Leasing Metrics (ALL REQUIRED)

| Metric | Benchmark | Why It Matters |
|--------|-----------|----------------|
| Occupancy Rate | >98% excellent (NNN); >95% good (shopping); <95% concerning | Revenue stability |
| Same-Store NOI Growth | 1-3% typical; >3% strong | Core property performance |
| Rent Spreads on Renewal | 5-15% strong; flat concerning; negative red flag | Pricing power |
| Leasing Volume | Track new vs. renewal activity | Demand indicator |
| Tenant Retention Rate | >80% healthy | Renewal success |

### Lease Structure Metrics (ALL REQUIRED)

| Metric | Benchmark | Why It Matters |
|--------|-----------|----------------|
| Weighted Average Lease Term (WALT) | 8-12 years healthy; <6 years concerning | Revenue visibility |
| Annual Rent Escalator | 1-3% built-in growth | Contractual increases |
| Investment Grade Tenant % | >60% strong; <40% credit risk | Tenant quality |
| Top 10 Tenant Concentration | <30% diversified; >40% concentrated | Revenue concentration |

### Financial Metrics (ALL REQUIRED)

| Metric | Benchmark | Why It Matters |
|--------|-----------|----------------|
| AFFO Per Share Growth | >3% strong; 1-3% adequate; flat/negative concerning | Value creation |
| AFFO Payout Ratio | 75-85% sustainable; >95% unsustainable | Distribution sustainability |
| Net Debt / EBITDA | <5x healthy (NNN); <6x healthy (shopping); >7x concerning | Leverage |
| Weighted Average Cost of Debt | Trending down preferred | Financing cost |
| Unencumbered Asset % | >70% strong | Financial flexibility |

## Required Quirks (Section 3)

### 1. Straight-Line Rent Accounting

- **What:** Rent escalations recognized evenly over lease term, not as cash received
- **Distortion:** Revenue higher than cash early in lease, lower later; creates timing difference
- **Adjustment:** Track cash rent vs. straight-line; understand impact on FFO vs. cash

### 2. Lease Termination Income

- **What:** Tenants paying to exit leases early creates one-time income
- **Distortion:** Termination fees spike income; may signal tenant weakness
- **Adjustment:** Strip termination income from recurring earnings; investigate why tenants leaving

### 3. Tenant Improvement Amortization

- **What:** TI costs capitalized and amortized over lease term; varies by company
- **Distortion:** TI-heavy leasing improves near-term margins but creates future drag
- **Adjustment:** Track TI spending trends; compare TI per lease to peers

### 4. Investment Grade Definition Variance

- **What:** Some REITs use actual ratings; others use "deemed" investment grade
- **Distortion:** "Deemed" IG may include non-rated tenants management considers creditworthy
- **Adjustment:** Understand company's IG definition; compare to peers consistently

## Valuation Guidance (Section 8)

### Primary Valuation Method

**Price-to-AFFO**

| Property Type | Justified P/AFFO | Rationale |
|---------------|------------------|-----------|
| NNN with IG tenants, long WALT | 16-22x | Premium for stability |
| Shopping centers, quality locations | 12-18x | More leasing risk |
| Value-add or transitioning | 10-14x | Execution risk |

### Secondary Methods

- **Cap Rate on NOI:** Compare to private market transactions
- **Dividend Yield:** 4-6% for quality NNN; higher for riskier
- **NAV (Net Asset Value):** Property value minus debt

### Methods to AVOID

| Method | Why Inappropriate |
|--------|-------------------|
| P/E (GAAP) | Depreciation makes meaningless |
| P/B | Book depreciation doesn't reflect asset value |
| FFO without maintenance capex | AFFO more accurate |
| EV/Revenue | Lease structure differences |

### DCF Considerations

- **Rent escalators:** Model contractual growth
- **Lease expirations:** Track rollover schedule
- **Tenant credit:** Default scenarios
- **Cap rate assumptions:** Exit cap critical

### Owner's Earnings Adjustments

```
FFO (Funds From Operations)                   $X,XXXM (FYxxxx)
- Straight-Line Rent Adjustment               ($XXXM) [timing difference]
- Maintenance CapEx                           ($XXXM) [recurring property investment]
- Lease Termination Income                    ($XXXM) [non-recurring]
+ Above/Below Market Rent Amortization         $XXXM [non-cash]
= AFFO (Adjusted FFO)                        ~$X,XXXM

Note: AFFO is the standard "Owner's Earnings" for retail REITs
```

## Peer Selection (Section 7)

### Match On

| Factor | Why It Matters |
|--------|----------------|
| Property type (NNN vs. shopping centers) | Different risk profiles |
| Tenant quality (IG concentration) | Credit risk |
| Lease duration (WALT) | Cash flow visibility |
| Geographic concentration | Regional economic exposure |
| Property size/format | Different tenant types |

### Flag as False Comparables

| Company Type | Why Not Comparable |
|--------------|-------------------|
| Office REITs | Different tenant dynamics |
| Industrial/warehouse | Different demand drivers |
| Mall operators | Higher risk, different economics |
| Mortgage REITs | Completely different model |
| Single-tenant operators | Concentration differences |

---

## Red Flags to Document

| Red Flag | What It Signals |
|----------|-----------------|
| Occupancy decline below 95% (NNN) | Tenant credit issues |
| Negative rent spreads | Pricing power eroding |
| AFFO payout ratio >95% sustained | No reinvestment capacity |
| Net debt/EBITDA >7x | Over-leveraged |
| Top tenant >15% of rent | Concentration risk |
| WALT declining below 7 years | Increasing lease risk |

---

## Green Flags to Document

| Green Flag | What It Signals |
|------------|-----------------|
| Occupancy >99% with positive spreads | Strong tenant demand |
| WALT >10 years | Long visibility |
| AFFO payout ratio <80% | Reinvestment capacity |
| Investment grade tenants >70% | Credit quality |
| Same-store NOI growth >2% | Pricing power |
