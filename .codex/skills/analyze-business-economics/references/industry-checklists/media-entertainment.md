## Required Quirks (Section 3)

### 1. Content Amortization Methods

- **What:** Film/TV content capitalized and amortized; methods vary (accelerated vs. straight-line)
- **Distortion:** Accelerated amortization front-loads costs; straight-line spreads evenly; not comparable
- **Adjustment:** Compare content costs as % of revenue; understand accounting policy differences

### 2. Film/TV Impairments

- **What:** Theatrical underperformance triggers impairments on capitalized content
- **Distortion:** Impairments are lumpy, one-time charges that hit earnings unpredictably
- **Adjustment:** Track impairment trends; exclude from normalized earnings

### 3. Affiliate Fee Revenue Recognition

- **What:** Multi-year affiliate deals with escalators recognized on various methods
- **Distortion:** Revenue timing may differ from cash; contract changes create step-ups
- **Adjustment:** Understand contract renewal timing; track cash vs. recognized revenue

### 4. Sports Rights Amortization

- **What:** Long-term sports rights capitalized and amortized over contract period
- **Distortion:** Large upfront cash payments spread over time; cash vs. P&L disconnect
- **Adjustment:** Track sports rights cash outflow separately; understand contract escalators

## Valuation Guidance (Section 8)

### Primary Valuation Method

**Sum-of-Parts by Segment**

| Segment | Valuation Method | Typical Multiple |
|---------|------------------|------------------|
| Streaming | EV/Sub or EV/Revenue with margin path | $200-500/sub; 3-8x revenue |
| Linear Networks | EBITDA multiple with decline | 4-8x declining EBITDA |
| Theme Parks | EBITDA multiple | 10-14x normalized EBITDA |
| Studio | EBITDA or revenue multiple | 8-12x EBITDA |

### Secondary Methods

- **Consolidated EV/EBITDA:** 8-14x depending on mix and growth
- **DCF by segment:** Sum individual segment DCFs
- **P/E:** Only at profitability across all segments

### Methods to AVOID

| Method | Why Inappropriate |
|--------|-------------------|
| Consolidated Multiples without context | Mix too different |
| Streaming subscriber multiple on losses | Must consider profitability path |
| P/E during transition | Streaming losses distort |
| Historical multiples | Business fundamentally changing |

### DCF Considerations

- **Streaming path to profit:** When does breakeven occur?
- **Linear decline modeling:** Rate of cord-cutting
- **Parks cyclicality:** Recession sensitivity
- **Content investment cycles:** Lumpy spending

### Owner's Earnings Adjustments

Operating Income                              $X,XXXM (FYxxxx)

## Peer Selection (Section 7)

### Match On

| Factor | Why It Matters |
|--------|----------------|
| Business mix (streaming, linear, parks) | Different economics |
| Streaming maturity | Growth vs. profitability phase |
| Content ownership vs. licensing | Margin structure |
| Geographic footprint | Market opportunities |

### Flag as False Comparables

| Company Type | Why Not Comparable |
|--------------|-------------------|
| Pure streamers (Netflix) vs. legacy media | Different transition |
| Theme park pure-plays vs. integrated | Different exposures |
| Broadcast vs. cable networks | Different dynamics |
| Sports leagues | Different model |
| Social media/tech | User-generated vs. professional content |

## Red Flags to Document

| Red Flag | What It Signals |
|----------|-----------------|
| Streaming subscriber churn accelerating | Product issues |
| Linear decline >8% annually | Cord-cutting accelerating |
| Content impairments recurring | Poor content decisions |
| DTC losses widening | Path to profit unclear |
| Parks per capita declining | Pricing resistance |
| Sports rights costs escalating faster than revenue | Margin pressure |

---

## Green Flags to Document

| Green Flag | What It Signals |
|------------|-----------------|
| Streaming ARPU growth with sub growth | Healthy monetization |
| DTC path to profitability clear | Business model viable |
| Parks per capita growing above inflation | Pricing power |
| Linear decline slowing | Stabilization |
| Content hits driving engagement | Franchise strength |
