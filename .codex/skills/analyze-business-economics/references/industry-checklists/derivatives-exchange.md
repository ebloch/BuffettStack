## Required Metrics (Section 4)

### Volume & Revenue Metrics (ALL REQUIRED)

| Metric | Benchmark | Why It Matters |
|--------|-----------|----------------|
| Average Daily Volume (ADV) | >25M contracts strong for diversified exchange | Scale and market position |
| Rate Per Contract (RPC) | Stable or growing; watch for compression | Pricing power indicator |
| Transaction Revenue Growth | >GDP growth strong | Core business momentum |
| Market Data Revenue Growth | >4% YoY healthy; >6% strong | High-margin recurring stream |
| Other Revenue % (non-transaction) | >30% diversified | Revenue durability |

### Profitability Metrics (ALL REQUIRED)

| Metric | Benchmark | Why It Matters |
|--------|-----------|----------------|
| Operating Margin | >63% excellent; 60-63% good; <60% concerning | Scale advantage indicator |
| EBITDA Margin | >67% excellent | Pure operating efficiency |
| Incremental Margin | >70% strong | Operating leverage on growth |
| FCF Conversion (FCF/NI) | >100% excellent; <90% investigate | Earnings quality |

### Capital Metrics (ALL REQUIRED)

| Metric | Benchmark | Why It Matters |
|--------|-----------|----------------|
| ROIC | >25% excellent; 20-25% good; <15% concerning | Capital efficiency |
| Dividend Payout Ratio | 60-80% sustainable; >90% may limit growth | Capital allocation |
| Capex / Revenue | 3-5% typical; low capital needs | Asset-light model |

## Required Quirks (Section 3)

### 1. Customer Margin Deposits (Performance Bonds)

- **What:** Clearing house holds $90-170B+ in customer collateral as performance bonds
- **Distortion:** Both assets and liabilities swing by tens of billions; inflates balance sheet massively
- **Adjustment:** Exclude customer cash and deposits from both sides of balance sheet; focus on operating assets only

### 2. Interest Income on Customer Cash

- **What:** Exchange earns interest on customer cash collateral; can be $500M+ annually
- **Distortion:** Gross interest income reported, but much shared with customers; net spread is what matters
- **Adjustment:** Track net interest earned (gross minus sharing); understand this is rate-sensitive revenue

### 3. Trading Product Intangibles

- **What:** Acquired exchanges carry intangibles for product rights (e.g., S&P index futures)
- **Distortion:** Indefinite-lived intangibles not amortized; may or may not reflect ongoing value
- **Adjustment:** These often represent genuine franchise value; verify licensing agreements are long-term

### 4. Equity Method Income

- **What:** Stakes in related entities (e.g., index providers, international exchanges) reported below operating line
- **Distortion:** Significant income stream ($100M+) appears non-operating
- **Adjustment:** Include equity method income in core earnings if strategic and recurring

## Valuation Guidance (Section 8)

### Primary Valuation Method

**P/E on Core Earnings**

| Market Position | Justified P/E | Rationale |
|-----------------|---------------|-----------|
| Monopoly products, global scale | 25-32x | Premium for irreplaceable position |
| Strong competitive position | 20-28x | Solid franchise value |
| Commoditized/competitive | 15-20x | More substitution risk |

### Secondary Methods

- **EV/EBITDA:** 16-22x for premium exchanges; useful for acquisition context
- **FCF Yield:** 3-5% yield for high-quality; strong generators
- **Dividend Yield:** Often valued partially as yield vehicle

### Methods to AVOID

| Method | Why Inappropriate |
|--------|-------------------|
| P/B | Balance sheet includes massive customer deposits; not meaningful |
| Net Debt Including Restricted Cash | Customer cash distorts; use corporate debt only |
| EV/Revenue | Margin differences across exchanges too significant |
| Raw Asset Multiples | Intangibles dominant; book value meaningless |

### DCF Considerations

- **Volume sensitivity:** Model scenarios for volatility-driven volume swings
- **RPC trends:** Assume modest compression unless pricing power evidence
- **Market data growth:** High-margin, often contractual, valuable stream
- **Interest rate sensitivity:** Interest on customer cash moves with rates

## Peer Selection (Section 7)

### Match On

| Factor | Why It Matters |
|--------|----------------|
| Product mix (derivatives vs. equities vs. data) | Different economics |
| Geographic footprint | Regional vs. global reach |
| Clearing presence | Risk profile and margin income |
| Market data revenue % | Recurring revenue mix |
| Regulatory environment | Compliance cost structures |

### Flag as False Comparables

| Company Type | Why Not Comparable |
|--------------|-------------------|
| Stock exchanges (equity-focused) | Different volume dynamics, margin |
| Brokers/dealers | Customer-facing vs. infrastructure |
| Market makers | Risk-taking vs. fee model |
| Fintech payment companies | Different regulatory and competitive dynamics |
| OTC trading venues | Different structure and economics |

## Red Flags to Document

| Red Flag | What It Signals |
|----------|-----------------|
| RPC declining >2% annually | Pricing pressure or mix shift |
| Volume share loss in core products | Competitive threat |
| Operating margin compression | Cost discipline issues |
| Market data revenue flat/declining | Contract renewals weakening |
| Regulatory pressure on clearing | Structural economics threat |
| Technology spend lagging | Infrastructure risk |

---

## Green Flags to Document

| Green Flag | What It Signals |
|------------|-----------------|
| Monopoly or duopoly in core products | Pricing power durability |
| Market data growth >5% CAGR | High-margin recurring expansion |
| Operating margin >65% | Scale advantage intact |
| New product launches gaining traction | Growth optionality |
| International expansion success | TAM expansion |
