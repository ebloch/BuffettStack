## Required Quirks (Section 3)

### 1. Gross vs Net Revenue (Client Incentives)

- **What:** Gross revenue reduced by massive client incentives ($10B+ for large networks)
- **Distortion:** Client incentives can be 25-30% of gross; growing incentives compress net revenue growth
- **Adjustment:** ALWAYS analyze net revenue after incentives; track incentive growth vs. volume growth

### 2. Client Incentive Accounting and Front-Loading

- **What:** Incentives recognized over contract life, but upfront payments common
- **Distortion:** Cash outflow timing differs from expense recognition; large renewals create cash drag
- **Adjustment:** Track cash incentives paid vs. recognized; watch for contract renewal years

### 3. FX Impact on Payment Volumes

- **What:** Volumes reported in USD; international volumes affected by currency translation
- **Distortion:** Reported volume growth can differ significantly from constant currency growth
- **Adjustment:** Track constant currency volume growth; understand geographic mix

### 4. Interchange vs Network Fees

- **What:** Networks earn network fees; interchange flows through to issuers
- **Distortion:** Networks don't keep interchange but it affects merchant acceptance
- **Adjustment:** Focus on network fees (service fees, data processing, international transaction fees)

## Valuation Guidance (Section 8)

### Primary Valuation Method

**P/E on Core Earnings**

| Market Position | Justified P/E | Rationale |
|-----------------|---------------|-----------|
| Duopoly leader, global scale | 28-38x | Premium for irreplaceable position |
| Strong position, growing | 22-30x | Quality growth franchise |
| Regional/niche player | 15-22x | More competitive exposure |

### Secondary Methods

- **EV/EBITDA:** 20-28x for premium networks
- **PEG Ratio:** 1.5-2.5x for growth networks
- **FCF Yield:** 2-4% yield for premium

### Methods to AVOID

| Method | Why Inappropriate |
|--------|-------------------|
| Gross Revenue Multiples | Must use net revenue |
| P/S without yield context | Net yield differences make revenue multiples misleading |
| Volume Multiples | Economics per dollar differ by geography and type |
| P/B | Intangibles and buybacks distort |

### DCF Considerations

- **Volume growth:** Track vs. GDP and digital penetration
- **Net revenue yield:** Assume modest compression unless pricing power clear
- **Operating leverage:** High incremental margins
- **Capex needs:** Low capital intensity; mainly technology

## Peer Selection (Section 7)

### Match On

| Factor | Why It Matters |
|--------|----------------|
| Business model (network vs. processor) | Different economics |
| Geographic footprint | Growth rates and competitive dynamics |
| Customer mix (consumer vs. commercial) | Different economics |
| Cross-border exposure | Revenue quality mix |
| Value-added services mix | Diversification |

### Flag as False Comparables

| Company Type | Why Not Comparable |
|--------------|-------------------|
| Payment processors (acquirers) | Different position in value chain |
| Fintech payment apps (PayPal) | Consumer-facing vs. infrastructure |
| Banks (issuing/acquiring) | Different business model |
| Regional debit networks | Different scale and economics |
| BNPL providers | Different risk and model |

## Red Flags to Document

| Red Flag | What It Signals |
|----------|-----------------|
| Net revenue yield declining | Pricing pressure or mix shift |
| Client incentives growing faster than volume | Competitive pressure |
| Cross-border growth slowing | High-margin segment weakening |
| Operating margin compression | Cost discipline or competitive issues |
| Volume growth below GDP | Market share loss |
| Regulatory action on interchange | Revenue at risk |

---

## Green Flags to Document

| Green Flag | What It Signals |
|------------|-----------------|
| Cross-border volume growth >15% | High-margin expansion |
| Net revenue yield stable or growing | Pricing power intact |
| Value-added services growth >20% | Diversification working |
| Operating margin >65% | Scale advantage |
| New acceptance expansion | TAM growth |
