## Required Metrics (Section 4)

### Volume Metrics (ALL REQUIRED)

| Metric | Benchmark | Why It Matters |
|--------|-----------|----------------|
| GMV Growth | 15-25% strong; 10-15% moderate; <10% maturing | Total transaction value |
| Take Rate (Revenue/GMV) | 10-15% healthy; watch for compression | Monetization intensity |
| Revenue vs GMV Growth | Revenue > GMV = take rate expansion | Monetization improvement |
| Active Buyers Growth | Positive; flat/declining concerning | Customer base expansion |
| Orders Per Buyer | Growing indicates engagement | Customer retention quality |

### Profitability Metrics (ALL REQUIRED)

| Metric | Benchmark | Why It Matters |
|--------|-----------|----------------|
| Contribution Margin | Improving trend expected | Unit economics trajectory |
| Marketing as % of Revenue | Declining as % at scale | Efficiency improvement |
| Adjusted EBITDA Margin | Path to 15-25% at maturity | Operating leverage |
| Free Cash Flow Margin | Positive at scale; can be negative during growth | Cash generation |

### Customer Metrics (ALL REQUIRED)

| Metric | Benchmark | Why It Matters |
|--------|-----------|----------------|
| Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) | Stable or declining | Efficiency |
| Cohort Revenue Retention | >100% indicates spend growth | Customer lifetime value |
| Repeat Purchase Rate | >60% for mature marketplace | Stickiness |

## Required Quirks (Section 3)

### 1. 1P vs 3P Revenue Recognition

- **What:** 1P (direct inventory sales) = gross revenue; 3P (marketplace) = commission only (net revenue)
- **Distortion:** 1P revenue 5-10x higher than 3P for same GMV; margins look completely different
- **Adjustment:** Compare GMV and take rates, not revenue; understand 1P vs 3P mix

### 2. Fintech/Credit Provisions

- **What:** Many marketplaces offer credit products; requires credit loss provisions
- **Distortion:** Growing credit book creates large provisions; masks marketplace profitability
- **Adjustment:** Analyze marketplace and fintech segments separately; understand credit portfolio growth

### 3. FX Translation (International Operations)

- **What:** LatAm and international operations subject to currency translation
- **Distortion:** Reported growth differs significantly from constant currency growth
- **Adjustment:** Track constant currency growth; understand hedging strategy

### 4. Deferred Revenue (Prepaid Services)

- **What:** Prepaid subscriptions, shipping credits recognized over time
- **Distortion:** Cash received before revenue recognized
- **Adjustment:** Track deferred revenue trends; understand timing of recognition

## Valuation Guidance (Section 8)

### Primary Valuation Method

**EV/Revenue with margin path** (for growth stage)

| Growth Rate + Path to Profit | Justified EV/Revenue | Rationale |
|------------------------------|----------------------|-----------|
| >30% GMV growth, improving take rate | 4-8x | High-growth with leverage |
| 15-30% GMV growth, clear profit path | 2-5x | Solid trajectory |
| <15% GMV growth | 1-3x | Maturing, value on earnings |

**EV/EBITDA** (for profitable stage)

| Margin + Growth | Justified EV/EBITDA | Rationale |
|-----------------|---------------------|-----------|
| >15% margin, >15% growth | 20-30x | Premium growth compounder |
| 10-15% margin, moderate growth | 12-20x | Solid business |
| <10% margin | 8-15x | Profitability concerns |

### Secondary Methods

- **Sum-of-Parts:** Marketplace + fintech + logistics + advertising valued separately
- **GMV Multiple:** Compare EV/GMV across peers
- **P/E:** At profitability; 25-40x for growth

### Methods to AVOID

| Method | Why Inappropriate |
|--------|-------------------|
| Revenue Multiple without 1P/3P context | Mix distorts |
| Comparing 1P and 3P companies directly | Different models |
| GAAP Earnings for loss-making | Not meaningful |
| P/B | Intangibles and losses distort |

### DCF Considerations

- **GMV growth trajectory:** Market penetration runway
- **Take rate evolution:** Monetization levers
- **Credit losses:** If fintech exposure
- **Marketing leverage:** CAC efficiency over time

### Owner's Earnings Adjustments

```
Adjusted EBITDA                               $X,XXXM (FYxxxx)
- Stock-Based Compensation                    ($XXXM) [real expense]
- Maintenance CapEx                           ($XXXM) [logistics infrastructure]
- Growth CapEx (optional - asset building)    ($XXXM) [warehouses, data centers]
+ Credit Loss Normalization                    $XXXM [if provision above expected]
= Owner's Earnings                           ~$X,XXXM

Note: For unprofitable marketplaces, focus on contribution margin progression
```

## Peer Selection (Section 7)

### Match On

| Factor | Why It Matters |
|--------|----------------|
| Business model (1P vs 3P vs hybrid) | Revenue recognition differs |
| Category (retail vs. travel vs. services) | Different economics |
| Geographic footprint | Market dynamics and competition |
| Fintech integration | Additional business line |
| Fulfillment model (3PL vs. owned) | Capital intensity |

### Flag as False Comparables

| Company Type | Why Not Comparable |
|--------------|-------------------|
| Traditional retailers | Different model |
| 1P e-commerce vs. pure marketplace | Revenue recognition |
| Software marketplaces | Different dynamics |
| Social commerce | Different funnel |
| Brick-and-mortar with online presence | Different economics |

## Red Flags to Document

| Red Flag | What It Signals |
|----------|-----------------|
| GMV growth slowing to <10% | Market saturation |
| Take rate compression | Competitive pressure |
| Active buyers declining | Customer attrition |
| Marketing % of revenue not declining | No operating leverage |
| Credit losses accelerating | Fintech risk |
| 1P inventory building | Demand issues |

---

## Green Flags to Document

| Green Flag | What It Signals |
|------------|-----------------|
| GMV growth >20% with take rate expansion | Strong monetization |
| Cohort retention improving | Customer loyalty |
| Contribution margin expanding | Unit economics improving |
| Advertising revenue growing | High-margin incremental |
| Fulfillment efficiency improving | Operating leverage |
