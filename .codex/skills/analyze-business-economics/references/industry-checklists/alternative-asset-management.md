## Required Metrics (Section 4)

### Fee Economics (ALL REQUIRED)

| Metric | Benchmark | Why It Matters |
|--------|-----------|----------------|
| Fee Related Earnings (FRE) | Track absolute growth and margin trend | Stable, predictable earnings base |
| FRE Margin | >55% excellent; 50-55% good; <50% concerning | Operating leverage on management fees |
| Fee-Earning AUM Growth | >8% YoY strong; 5-8% adequate; <5% concerning | Revenue growth driver |
| Perpetual Capital % | >40% excellent; 30-40% good; <25% concerning | Fee duration and stability |
| Blended Management Fee Rate | 0.90-1.00% healthy; 0.85-0.90% acceptable; <0.85% signals compression | Pricing power indicator |

### Performance & Capital (ALL REQUIRED)

| Metric | Benchmark | Why It Matters |
|--------|-----------|----------------|
| Net Accrued Performance Revenues | Track as pipeline indicator | Future carry crystallization |
| Realized Performance Revenue / DE | 10-30% typical; >40% elevated vintage | Earnings quality mix |
| Distributable Earnings (DE) | More stable than GAAP net income | Cash available for distribution |
| DE Payout Ratio | 80-90% sustainable; >100% unsustainable | Distribution sustainability |
| Dry Powder | Growing but not excessive | Deployment capacity |

## Required Quirks (Section 3)

### 1. Unrealized Performance in GAAP Earnings

- **What:** GAAP consolidates unrealized investment gains and accrued carry into net income
- **Distortion:** Net income can swing 50%+ based on quarterly mark-to-market of portfolio; massive volatility
- **Adjustment:** Focus on Fee Related Earnings (FRE) and Distributable Earnings (DE); ignore GAAP NI

### 2. Fund Consolidation

- **What:** GAAP requires consolidation of certain funds, inflating both assets and liabilities
- **Distortion:** Balance sheet assets appear 5-10x larger than economic reality; creates confusion
- **Adjustment:** Use "total segment assets" or deconsolidated view; understand what manager actually owns

### 3. Net Accrued Performance Revenues

- **What:** Unrealized carried interest that would be earned if funds liquidated today
- **Distortion:** Shows as asset/revenue but can reverse with market declines; not guaranteed
- **Adjustment:** Track as leading indicator only; value primarily on FRE; apply discount to accrued carry

### 4. Perpetual vs Drawdown Fee Recognition

- **What:** Perpetual capital earns fees continuously; drawdown funds earn on committed then invested capital
- **Distortion:** Revenue timing differs significantly between capital types
- **Adjustment:** Track perpetual capital % and deployment pace separately; model fee revenue by capital type

## Valuation Guidance (Section 8)

### Primary Valuation Method

**Price-to-FRE (Fee Related Earnings)**

| FRE Growth + Margin | Justified P/FRE | Rationale |
|---------------------|-----------------|-----------|
| >15% FRE growth, >55% margin | 25-35x | Premium for scale and growth |
| 10-15% FRE growth, >50% margin | 20-25x | Solid growth platform |
| <10% FRE growth | 15-20x | Mature, lower growth |

### Secondary Methods

- **Sum-of-Parts:** FRE multiple + discounted accrued carry + GP stakes at NAV
- **DE Yield:** For mature platforms, 4-6% yield sustainable
- **AUM Multiple:** $20-40M per $1B FE-AUM for quality platforms

### Methods to AVOID

| Method | Why Inappropriate |
|--------|-------------------|
| GAAP P/E | Massively distorted by unrealized gains/losses |
| P/B | Book value includes fund consolidation; meaningless |
| EV/EBITDA | EBITDA includes unrealized performance; debt includes fund-level |
| EV/Revenue | Revenue includes unrealized; not comparable |

### Owner's Earnings Adjustments

```
Distributable Earnings                        $X,XXXM (FYxxxx)
- Stock-Based Compensation                    ($XXXM) [real expense]
- Principal Investment Gains (if included)    ($XXXM) [volatile, exclude]
+ Unrealized Performance Amortization            $XXXM [add back if DE excludes]
= Adjusted Owner's Earnings                  ~$X,XXXM

Note: FRE is the cleanest proxy; DE adds realized carry which is lumpy
```

## Peer Selection (Section 7)

### Match On

| Factor | Why It Matters |
|--------|----------------|
| Strategy mix (PE/credit/real estate/infra) | Different economics and cycles |
| Perpetual capital % | Fee stability and duration |
| FRE margin profile | Operating leverage comparability |
| Geographic and investor base mix | Different growth trajectories |
| Track record vintage | Performance revenue timing |

### Flag as False Comparables

| Company Type | Why Not Comparable |
|--------------|-------------------|
| Traditional asset managers (mutual funds) | Lower fees, no carry, different model |
| Hedge funds (pure) | Different fee structure, AUM volatility |
| Wealth managers | Fee-only, no investment income |
| Investment banks | Trading vs. investment management |
| BDCs/funds | Investment vehicle vs. manager |

## Red Flags to Document

| Red Flag | What It Signals |
|----------|-----------------|
| FRE margin declining trend | Cost creep or fee pressure |
| Fee rate compression >10bps/year | Competitive pressure |
| Fundraising pace slowing materially | LP appetite declining |
| Net accrued carry declining | Performance issues emerging |
| Key person departures | Franchise risk |
| DE payout ratio >100% sustained | Unsustainable distribution |

---

## Green Flags to Document

| Green Flag | What It Signals |
|------------|-----------------|
| Perpetual capital >50% of FE-AUM | Fee stability and longevity |
| FRE margin expanding with scale | Operating leverage working |
| Multi-decade track record top quartile | Fundraising power |
| Diversified across strategies | Reduced cyclicality |
| Strong co-investment capacity | Alignment and upside |
