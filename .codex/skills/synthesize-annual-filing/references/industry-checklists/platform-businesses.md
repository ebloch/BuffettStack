# Asset-Light / Platform Businesses — Mandatory Checklist

**CRITICAL:** Every item must be addressed (found and included, OR explicitly noted as "not disclosed").

## Key Metrics

- [ ] GMV/GTV and take rate (or equivalent: ADV and RPC for exchanges, Gross Bookings for OTAs)
- [ ] **Revenue vs. GMV growth differential (REQUIRED)** — Calculate and explicitly state: "Revenue grew X% vs. GMV Y% = Z bps take rate expansion/compression." Include in Key Takeaways or Watch Items.
- [ ] Network metrics (buyers, sellers, transactions; properties, room nights for OTAs; open interest for exchanges)
- [ ] Unit economics (contribution margin per transaction) — If not disclosed, state "Unit economics: Not disclosed"
- [ ] Geographic expansion metrics
- [ ] **Take rate / RPC trend (REQUIRED)** — Include 3-year history in Quick Reference or Watch Items table. Format: "Take rate: 14.3% (2024), 14.2% (2023), 14.1% (2022)". Search MD&A "Results of Operations" for prior year comparisons. If only YoY available, state that explicitly.

## Watch Items (Flag If Moving in Concerning Direction)

- [ ] Take rate declining (competitive pressure)
- [ ] GMV growing faster than revenue (take rate compression)
- [ ] Buyer/seller ratio imbalanced
- [ ] Unit economics deteriorating
- [ ] High geographic concentration with expansion failures

## Exchanges / Clearing Houses (If Applicable)

### Exchange-Specific Metrics
- [ ] **Average Daily Volume (ADV)** by product class with YoY change
- [ ] **Rate per Contract (RPC)** with 3-year trend — this is the exchange "take rate"
- [ ] **Open Interest** as network depth indicator
- [ ] **Electronic volume %** — shows transition from open outcry
- [ ] **Member vs. non-member volume %** — indicates pricing power constraints
- [ ] **Volume by geography/trading session** — European, APAC hours growth

### Clearing House Metrics
- [ ] **Clearing fund size** — guaranty fund plus member contributions
- [ ] **Collateral held** — total margin and guaranty fund
- [ ] **Default waterfall structure** — described in Risk Register
- [ ] **Regulatory designation** — SIFMU, DCO, DCM, etc.
- [ ] **Capital requirements** — specific regulatory capital ratios

### Exchange Watch Items
- [ ] RPC declining (member mix shift, volume discounts, competition)
- [ ] Volume concentration in few products (product risk)
- [ ] New exchange competition (e.g., FMX Futures) — flag as `[NEW]` if emerging threat
- [ ] Technology migration risk (cloud, co-location) — flag if material

## Online Travel Agencies / Marketplaces (If Applicable)

### OTA-Specific Metrics
- [ ] **Gross Bookings (GTV)** — total transaction value with YoY change
- [ ] **Take Rate** — Revenue / Gross Bookings as percentage. MUST show 3-year trend or note "Prior year data not available"
- [ ] **Room Nights / Transaction Volume** — primary volume metric with YoY
- [ ] **Average Daily Rate (ADR)** — if disclosed, include in Operating Metrics
- [ ] **Direct Booking Mix** — % of bookings coming directly vs. paid channels (marketing efficiency indicator)
- [ ] **Mobile App Mix** — % of bookings via mobile app (stickiness indicator)
- [ ] **Merchant vs. Agency Mix** — revenue model shift indicator (merchant = payment facilitation)
- [ ] **Supply Metrics** — properties, listings, or partners on platform
- [ ] **Alternative Accommodation Mix** — for accommodation OTAs, % of non-hotel listings

### OTA Watch Items
- [ ] Take rate declining (competitive pressure, parity requirements)
- [ ] ADR growth slowing (market conditions, mix shift)
- [ ] Paid acquisition increasing vs. direct (marketing efficiency degrading)
- [ ] Merchant costs growing faster than merchant revenue
- [ ] Google/Meta vertical integration expanding

### OTA Marketing Efficiency (If Disclosed)
- [ ] **Marketing spend as % of revenue** — with YoY trend. If declining while direct mix stable, efficiency improving
- [ ] **Sales & marketing expense growth vs. revenue growth** — Should grow slower than revenue over time
- [ ] **CAC/Customer acquisition trends** — If disclosed or calculable from performance marketing spend / new customers
