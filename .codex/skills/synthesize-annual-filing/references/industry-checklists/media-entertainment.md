# Media / Entertainment — Mandatory Checklist

**CRITICAL:** Every item must be addressed (found and included, OR explicitly noted as "not disclosed").

**Applies to:** Streaming companies, studios, linear TV networks, parks/experiences operators, diversified entertainment conglomerates (Disney, Comcast, WBD, Paramount, Netflix, etc.)

---

## Streaming / DTC Metrics

- [ ] **Subscriber count** by service (e.g., Disney+ Core, Hotstar, Hulu, ESPN+, Netflix, Max)
- [ ] **Subscriber growth** YoY and QoQ with trend direction
- [ ] **ARPU (Average Revenue Per User)** by service or region — search "average monthly revenue per paid subscriber" or "ARPU"
- [ ] **Churn rate** if disclosed — search "churn", "cancellation", "subscriber retention"
- [ ] **DTC segment profitability** — operating income/loss trend, path to profitability
- [ ] **Advertising vs. subscription revenue split** for ad-supported tiers
- [ ] **International vs. domestic subscriber mix**

### Streaming Watch Items
- [ ] ARPU declining (price competition, ad-tier cannibalization)
- [ ] Subscriber growth decelerating
- [ ] Churn increasing
- [ ] DTC losses persisting or widening

---

## Linear TV Networks

- [ ] **Affiliate revenue** and YoY trend — search "affiliate fees", "carriage fees"
- [ ] **Advertising revenue** and YoY trend
- [ ] **Subscriber universe** (e.g., ESPN has 70M domestic subs) — search "Nielsen", "subscribers"
- [ ] **Key network ratings** if disclosed
- [ ] **Retransmission consent expiration dates** if disclosed

### Linear Watch Items
- [ ] Affiliate revenue declining (cord-cutting)
- [ ] Advertising revenue declining (secular shift to digital)
- [ ] Subscriber universe contracting

---

## Content Investment & Amortization

- [ ] **Content assets** on balance sheet — total and YoY change
- [ ] **Content amortization** — search "content expense", "program rights amortization", "produced and licensed content"
- [ ] **Content impairments** — search "content impairment", "content write-off"
- [ ] **Annual content spend** — guidance or actual
- [ ] **Theatrical release count** — number of films released
- [ ] **Streaming originals** — investment trend

*Content amortization is a major non-cash expense that distorts GAAP earnings. Extract for Owner's Earnings calculation.*

### Content Watch Items
- [ ] Content spend growing faster than revenue
- [ ] Recurring content impairments (poor investment decisions)
- [ ] Theatrical box office underperformance

---

## Parks & Experiences (If Applicable)

- [ ] **Attendance** — domestic and international with YoY change
- [ ] **Per-capita guest spending** — search "per capita", "guest spending" (admission + merchandise + F&B)
- [ ] **Hotel occupancy** — search "occupancy"
- [ ] **Parks CapEx** — maintenance vs. expansion
- [ ] **Cruise ships** — fleet size and expansion plans
- [ ] **Consumer products licensing revenue**

### Parks Watch Items
- [ ] Attendance declining
- [ ] Per-capita spending declining (consumer elasticity reached)
- [ ] Occupancy declining
- [ ] Margin compression from labor costs

---

## Sports Rights (If Applicable)

- [ ] **Major sports rights held** (NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL, college, soccer, etc.)
- [ ] **Rights expiration dates** — search "sports programming agreement", "rights agreement"
- [ ] **Annual sports rights spend**
- [ ] **JV arrangements** (e.g., ESPN/Fox/WBD Venu Sports)

### Sports Watch Items
- [ ] Rights costs escalating faster than revenue
- [ ] Losing key rights in renewal
- [ ] JV litigation or breakup risk

---

## Intellectual Property

- [ ] **Key IP expiration/public domain risks** — search "copyright", "public domain"
- [ ] **Major franchise performance** — Marvel, Star Wars, Pixar, DC, Harry Potter, etc.
- [ ] **Licensing revenue trends**

---

## Thesis-Critical Regulatory Risks (ACTIVELY SEARCH FOR)

- [ ] **FCC ownership rules** — search "FCC", "broadcast", "ownership rules"
- [ ] **Net neutrality / broadband regulation** — for cable/ISP operators
- [ ] **International content quotas** — EU, Canada, etc.
- [ ] **Sports betting regulation** — affects ESPN
- [ ] **AI and content production** — search "artificial intelligence", "AI", "generative"

---

## Goodwill & Impairment Risk

Media companies often carry substantial goodwill from acquisitions. Actively assess impairment risk:

- [ ] **Goodwill by segment** — search "goodwill" in segment disclosure
- [ ] **Fair value vs. carrying value cushion** — if disclosed (e.g., "<10% cushion")
- [ ] **Impairment sensitivity** — discount rate or cash flow changes that trigger impairment
- [ ] **Recent impairments** — FY and prior year amounts

*Large goodwill with thin cushions is a significant headline risk.*
