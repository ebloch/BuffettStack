# Berkshire Hathaway Inc. - Scuttlebutt

**Scuttlebutt Health Score:** 3.5 / 5.0  
**Assessment:** Mixed  
**Analysis Date:** 2026-05-24

---

## Key Takeaways

- **Parent trust remains the core strength:** Outside evidence still validates Berkshire as a conservative, permanent-capital owner with unusual balance-sheet strength.

- **Customer voice is subsidiary-specific:** GEICO, BNSF and BHE have different customer realities; switching intent is visible in GEICO complaints, while BNSF and BHE customers often face network or regulatory lock-in.

- **Employee sentiment is uneven:** Berkshire parent-level reviews are positive, but BNSF scores weakly on work-life balance and management, and GEICO forums point to workload and service-pressure concerns.

- **Partner evidence is modestly positive:** BNSF names Hobby Lobby and Darigold as customer-collaboration examples, but most partner evidence is company-curated.

- **The moat is partially validated:** Scale, liquidity, reputation and permanence are validated; local service quality and regulated-asset risk are the main challenges.

- **The largest red flag is BHE/PacifiCorp:** Wildfire litigation, settlements and rate recovery can turn a regulated utility from steady compounder into capital sink.

- **Bottom line:** The grassroots record supports owning Berkshire as a financial-strength compounder, while demanding closer monitoring of operating-unit execution under Greg Abel.

## Red Flags to Monitor

- [ ] **PacifiCorp wildfire/rate recovery:** Watch new settlements above $2.0B cumulative after reserves, adverse court outcomes, or denied cost recovery.
- [ ] **GEICO service quality during growth:** Watch claims satisfaction below 680/1,000 or combined ratio above 95.0%.
- [ ] **BNSF labor and service strain:** Watch employee work-life scores below 2.5/5 and operating performance lagging Union Pacific for two years.
- [ ] **Post-Buffett capital allocation drift:** Watch repurchases, acquisitions or subsidiary interventions that conflict with Berkshire's stated owner discipline.
